The whole game in three rules

One tap = one turn

The ball zigzags on its own. A tap reverses its direction. That is the game's only move.

Pink kills, grey bounces

Touch pink and the run is over. Grey obstacles bounce you back — for free.

Every tap counts

You start with 10 taps. Clearing obstacles without tapping earns them back. One tap breaks the streak.

The TEN challenge: the minimum is proven

18 levels. 10 taps. Not one more. Every level has a minimum number of taps — and that minimum is not an estimate: it is proven by a solver, level by level.

Finishing is one thing. Finishing at par is another. Now match it.

Five ways to play

Classic

A brand-new endless world every run. Taps can be earned back by playing clean: this is the world-leaderboard mode.

Daily run

The same world for everyone for twenty-four hours. Three official attempts; your best one counts.

Duels

Challenge a player on an identical world. Their score stays hidden until the verdict — 48 hours to play.

The Crossing

A campaign of 11 sectors and 91 missions: gates to break through, modifiers, secret objectives.

TEN

Ten taps, not one more. A free run for the record — and the 18 proven-par levels, yours to match.

Why you'll be back

  • Beat your record — every ranked run is replayed and verified by the server, move by move: what the leaderboard says is what real fingers really did.
  • The daily meet-up — a podium that closes every night. Tomorrow, it all resets.
  • The rematch — in a duel, same walls, same traps: only your decisions differ.
  • The Crossing — sectors to open, gates to break through, at your own pace.
  • The Collection — 48 badges, 32 emblems, styles for your ball and your trail. Only by playing: nothing is for sale.
