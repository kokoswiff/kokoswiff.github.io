TENFLUX is played entirely offline. It asks for no account, no name and no email address. If you play, it creates an anonymous identifier for you so your score can appear on a leaderboard, and it shows an ad between runs.

1. What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, GPS location, contacts, photos, files or messages. TENFLUX requests none of the corresponding permissions and has no field to receive them.

There is no analytics and no crash reporting in TENFLUX.

2. The player identifier

When you finish your first run, TENFLUX creates an anonymous account with Firebase Authentication (Google). That account has no username, no password and no email address: it is a number.

Until you have finished a run, none of this exists: opening the game, looking at it and closing it again creates no account.

Attached to that number are:

  • an automatically generated nickname (for example FLUX · 7K3Q) — you do not choose it, and it is the only text other players ever see of you;
  • the date the account was created and the date of your last session;
  • your submitted runs: distance, duration, mode, ruleset version, and the exact sequence of your taps.

Why the sequence of your taps. TENFLUX replays every submitted run on its server to check that the reported score matches what was actually played. That is what protects the leaderboard from cheating. This data has no other use and is never shown to anyone.

This identifier belongs to the installation. If you uninstall TENFLUX or change phone, it is lost and cannot be recovered.

3. Google sign-in

This version of TENFLUX does not offer sign-in with a Google account, or any other form of registration. If such a feature is ever added, this policy will be updated before it goes live.

4. Advertising

TENFLUX shows an interstitial ad between some runs, served by Google AdMob.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares, on its own account:

  • your IP address, from which an approximate location is derived;
  • your interactions with the ad;
  • diagnostic information;
  • device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID.

This data is used for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. It is sent to Google, which is the controller for it. Google publishes its own description of this collection at developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Android settings, at any time, without going through TENFLUX.

There is no banner, no rewarded video and no app-open ad. Daily and Levels show no ads at all, and no ad ever grants any gameplay advantage.

Your consent

If you are in a region that requires it (European Economic Area, United Kingdom and others), a Google consent screen is shown before any advertising. You can change your choice at any time: Settings → privacy options, inside the game.

That form is Google's own (User Messaging Platform); TENFLUX does not replace it and never guesses your country.

5. Anti-cheat verification

Runs submitted to the leaderboard are replayed on our server, as described in section 2. That is our only verification mechanism.

TENFLUX does not currently use device attestation (Google Play Integrity, Firebase App Check). Our server knows how to receive one, but the app sends none, so no data of that kind is collected. If this changes, this policy will be updated beforehand.

6. What stays on your phone

Your best score, your recent runs, your level progress, your settings and gameplay telemetry are stored on your phone only, in a private application folder. They are never transmitted.

They are deleted when you uninstall TENFLUX.

7. Security and transfers

Every exchange between TENFLUX and its server goes over HTTPS, without exception: the app refuses any other address. Google's advertising SDK likewise encrypts its traffic with TLS.

The TENFLUX server and its database are hosted on Google Cloud in Belgium (europe-west1). The database is reachable by no client: not the game, not a browser, not a signed-in player — only the TENFLUX server reaches it.

Google, as the provider of authentication, hosting and advertising, may process data outside the European Union under the terms of its own policies.

8. Retention

  • Player identifier, nickname, submitted runs: kept for as long as the leaderboard exists.
  • Server logs: they contain a hash of your identifier, never the identifier itself, and are kept for Google Cloud's default retention period.
  • Data on your phone: until you uninstall.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object to the processing of data concerning you.

The procedure is described on the data deletion page. In short: write to swiffswiffer@gmail.com quoting your in-game nickname (visible on the profile screen, for example FLUX · 7K3Q) — it is the only information that lets us find your record.

You may also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country.

10. Children

TENFLUX is not directed at children. The game is intended for an audience aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data concerning a child. If you believe this has happened, write to swiffswiffer@gmail.com and we will delete the record.

11. Changes

Any change to this policy will be published at this address, with a new date at the top of the page. Significant changes will be noted in the release notes of the update concerned.
