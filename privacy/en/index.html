TENFLUX is played without an account: no sign-up, no password. If you play, it creates an anonymous identifier and a public nickname — which you can personalise — so your score can appear on a leaderboard, and it shows an ad between runs. If you wish, you can link a Google account to protect and recover your profile (section 3). Gameplay works offline; the leaderboards, the daily, the duels and the account features use our online services.

1. What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, phone number, date of birth, GPS location, contacts, photos, files or messages. TENFLUX requests none of the corresponding permissions. The only free text you can type is your public nickname (section 2) — do not put your real name or any personal information in it.

Your email address is not stored by TENFLUX. If you link a Google account (section 3), it is processed by Firebase Authentication (Google), the service that handles sign-in, and appears neither in our database, nor on the leaderboards, nor in our logs.

There is no analytics in TENFLUX. When the application hits an internal error, it may send our server a minimal technical report, described precisely in section 6 — no free text, no advertising identifier, and nothing from your runs.

2. The player identifier

The first time you use a feature that needs TENFLUX's online services — starting a run, choosing a nickname, or opening the leaderboard or the daily — TENFLUX creates an anonymous account with Firebase Authentication (Google). Until you protect it (section 3), that account has no sign-in username, no password and no email address: it is a number.

Until you use one of those features, none of this exists: opening the game, looking at it and closing it again creates no account.

Attached to that number are:

  • a public nickname — generated automatically (for example FLUX · 7K3Q) until you choose one, and which you can type or change yourself in the game. It is the only text other players ever see of you: do not put any personal information in it;
  • the date the account was created and the date of your last session;
  • your submitted runs: distance, duration, mode, ruleset version, and the exact sequence of your taps;
  • depending on the features you use: your emblem (a visual chosen among the game's own, visible on the leaderboards), your collection (in-game badges and cosmetics) and the list of players you follow (MY PLAYERS — invisible to everyone else, including the players you follow);
  • depending on the features you use: your duels — whom you challenged or who challenged you (by nickname), your rounds and their distances, the result and your record (wins, losses, draws) — and the list of players whose challenges you refuse (visible to you alone). This data serves to organise and settle the duel, nothing else. Until a duel is over, your opponent sees how many rounds you have played, never your distances; the sequence of your taps is shown to no one, duels included.

Why the sequence of your taps. TENFLUX replays every submitted run on its server to check that the reported score matches what was actually played. That is what protects the leaderboard from cheating. This data has no other use and is never shown to anyone.

Without a linked Google account, this identifier belongs to the installation: if you uninstall TENFLUX or change phone without having protected your profile, it is lost. A protected profile can be recovered from “I already have an account” (section 3).

3. Google account (optional)

TENFLUX is played entirely without an account. If you wish, you can link your Google account to your profile (“Protect my account”): your TENFLUX identifier stays exactly the same, and you can then recover your profile on another phone (“I already have an account”) — nickname, verified best, level progress and the state of the daily as the server knows them.

Your email address is not stored by TENFLUX. It is processed by Firebase Authentication (Google), the service that handles sign-in, and appears neither in our database, nor on the leaderboards, nor in our logs. Firebase Authentication may process this data outside the European Union, under the terms of Google Cloud's contractual clauses.

4. Advertising

TENFLUX shows an interstitial ad between some runs, served by Google AdMob.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares, on its own account:

  • your IP address, from which an approximate location is derived;
  • your interactions with the ad;
  • diagnostic information;
  • device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID.

This data is used for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. It is sent to Google, which is the controller for it. Google publishes its own description of this collection at developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Android settings, at any time, without going through TENFLUX.

There is no banner, no rewarded video and no app-open ad. Levels show no ads at all. In the daily, an ad may be shown before each attempt — never during play. No ad ever grants any gameplay advantage.

Your consent

If you are in a region that requires it (European Economic Area, United Kingdom and others), a Google consent screen is shown before any advertising. You can change your choice at any time: Settings → privacy options, inside the game.

That form is Google's own (User Messaging Platform); TENFLUX does not replace it and never guesses your country.

5. Anti-cheat verification

Runs submitted to the leaderboard are replayed on our server, as described in section 2. That is our only verification mechanism.

TENFLUX does not currently use device attestation (Google Play Integrity, Firebase App Check). Our server knows how to receive one, but the app sends none, so no data of that kind is collected. If this changes, this policy will be updated beforehand.

6. Technical error reports

When TENFLUX hits an internal error — a screen that fails to build, an unexpected exception — the application records a technical report on your phone, and may then send a summary of it to our server, at most once per play session, and never during a run.

That summary contains exactly the following fields, and nothing else:

  • a grouping fingerprint (eight characters, derived from the error type and the code location);
  • the technical type of the error (for example StateError);
  • the net that caught it (startup, rendering, background task);
  • the application's version and build number;
  • the number of occurrences in the session;
  • the timestamp;
  • up to eight code locations (source file names of the game, such as home_screen.dart:42).

The report contains neither the error's detailed message (which stays on your phone), nor your nickname, nor your email address, nor your advertising identifier, nor anything from your runs or your save: those fields do not exist in the transmitted format. The transmission uses the same encrypted, authenticated connection as the rest of the game; in our logs the report is tied to a pseudonymous hash of your player identifier — never the identifier itself. It is therefore not fully anonymous while it is kept, and we prefer saying so to claiming otherwise.

Purpose: detecting, grouping and fixing application failures. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping a game that works — the data is reduced to the strict technical minimum, and you can object to this processing at any time by writing to privacy@tenflux.fr. Recipient: our server, hosted by Google Cloud in Belgium (section 8); the reports live in our technical logs and nowhere else — no third-party analytics, no crash platform. Retention: 30 days, then automatic deletion by log rotation. We can switch this transmission off on our side, at any time, for every player at once.

7. What stays on your phone

Your best score, your recent runs, your settings and gameplay telemetry stay on your phone, in a private application folder. Your level progress — and, depending on the app version, your collection — is additionally copied to our servers when a connection exists, so it can be restored if you recover your account (section 3).

Local data is deleted when you uninstall TENFLUX.

Android backup. If Android's automatic backup is enabled on your phone, your local game file (best distance, settings, progression) may be copied by the system into your own Google Drive and restored when you change device. That is your backup, not ours: we have no access to it.

Your sign-in session and your advertising choices are excluded from it by the app: they stay tied to this installation. Changing phone therefore carries neither your account — for that you need to have linked it to Google (point 3) — nor the advertising consent you gave, which will be asked again.

8. Security and transfers

Every exchange between TENFLUX and its server goes over HTTPS, without exception: the app refuses any other address. Google's advertising SDK likewise encrypts its traffic with TLS.

The TENFLUX server and its database are hosted on Google Cloud in Belgium (europe-west1). The database is reachable by no client: not the game, not a browser, not a signed-in player — only the TENFLUX server reaches it.

Google, as the provider of authentication, hosting and advertising, may process data outside the European Union. Those transfers are covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses and by the commitments Google publishes in its own data processing terms.

9. Retention

  • Player identifier, nickname, submitted runs, duels, level progress: kept for as long as the leaderboard exists, or until you delete your account (section 10) — verified runs are then kept without your identifier.
  • Server logs: they contain a hash of your identifier, never the identifier itself, and are kept for 30 days (the configured retention of our Google Cloud logs).
  • Technical error reports (section 6): 30 days, in the same logs, then automatic deletion.
  • Infrastructure logs: our host (Google Cloud) records the requests the server receives, which includes your IP address. These logs serve the operation and the security of the service, we do not link them to your profile, and they are kept for Google Cloud's default retention period.
  • Data on your phone: until you uninstall.

We do not set a period after which an inactive profile would be deleted automatically: a leaderboard only means something if it keeps the scores. You can, however, request the deletion of your own account at any time, from the app (section 10).

10. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object to the processing of data concerning you.

From the PROFILE → ACCOUNT screen, you can at any time request the deletion of your account — profile, nickname, emblem, leaderboard entries, daily history, level progress and, depending on the features you use, collection, MY PLAYERS list, duels, duel record and ignored-players list. A duel in progress is cancelled; a finished one stops carrying your nickname. Deletion is engaged immediately: your account is locked on the spot and nothing can be written to it any more, then the full server-side cleanup completes in the background, usually within minutes. The app hands you a deletion receipt and lets you check its completion. Verified run records are kept without your identifier (anti-cheat, see section 2).

You can also export a copy of your data (a readable JSON file) directly from the app: PROFILE → ACCOUNT → EXPORT MY DATA. A request by email remains possible at the address below.

The full procedure is described on the data deletion page. For a request by email, write to privacy@tenflux.fr quoting your in-game nickname (visible on the profile screen, for example FLUX · 7K3Q) — it is used only to find the record. For a Google-protected account the request is verified with a one-time code sent to the Google address linked to the account: a nickname alone never triggers a deletion or a data delivery.

You may also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country.

11. Children

TENFLUX is not directed at children. The game is intended for an audience aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data concerning a child. If you believe this has happened, write to privacy@tenflux.fr and we will delete the record.

12. What we rely on

To be complete in the GDPR's own vocabulary, here is the legal basis for each processing:

  • Player identifier, pseudonym, submitted runs, leaderboards, duels, progression, collection: performance of the service you asked for — there is no world leaderboard without an identifier and a score.
  • Anti-cheat replay and the anonymised retention of runs: our legitimate interest in a leaderboard that is not rigged.
  • Google account: your consent, given by pressing "Protect my account" — optional, and withdrawn by deleting the account.
  • Advertising: your consent, collected by the Google form described in point 4, which you can change at any time.
  • Infrastructure logs: our legitimate interest in operating and securing the service.
  • Technical error reports (section 6): our legitimate interest in detecting and fixing application failures — with a right to object, exercisable by email at any time.

13. Changes

Any change to this policy will be published at this address, with a new date at the top of the page. Significant changes will be noted in the release notes of the update concerned.
